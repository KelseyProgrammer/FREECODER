HALATION
Feedback Pitch Ecosystem
by Ament Audio  ·  v0.0.3  ·  VST3  ·  AU  ·  macOS

Feed your signal into 2–8 parallel pitch-shifted delay paths, each tuned to a configurable interval and fed back into itself at a controlled rate. At zero feedback, HALATION is a harmonically-rich doubler — stacked chords blooming outward in time. Turn up the feedback and it becomes a living wall of sound, breathing and sustaining on its own. Push further and the plugin becomes an instrument — a self-generating ambient texture that evolves long after you've released the keys.

Named after the photographic effect where light bleeds outward from its source. The sound should feel exactly like that.

What it does
Phase Vocoder Pitch Shift
FFT-based pitch shifting per path — 2048-sample FFT, 75% overlap-add, Hann windowed. Smooth, musical transposition without artifacts.
2–8 Parallel Paths
Each path runs an independent pitch-shifted delay with its own feedback loop. Add or remove paths without clicks or resets.
Interval Presets
Fifths, Octaves, Harmonic Series, Chromatic, Unison — or dial in any interval manually per path. Snaps to Custom when you deviate.
Bloom Visualizer
Radial canvas showing each active path as a bloom arm — length encodes interval, opacity follows level. Drag tips to tune intervals live.
Spectral Tilt
Per-cycle brightness control in the feedback path. Positive = each repeat gets brighter. Negative = darker, warmer, tape-style decay.
Chaos / Drift
Per-path slow LFO (0.1–0.5 Hz) that offsets pitch by ±15 cents, phase-staggered across voices. Organic, living intonation.
Stagger
Delay offset between paths (20–180 ms at full stagger). Spreads arrivals in time, turning chords into slow blooms.
DC Blocking + Damping
1-pole highpass at 10 Hz kills DC buildup in long feedback tails. Damping adds a gentle LP per cycle for analog warmth.
